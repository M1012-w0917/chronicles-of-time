- `file://<repo>/rewrite-v2/index.html`

## Interaction recorded and asserted (`tests/scenario-v2.mjs`)

Before rewrite, hold Space and release early (cancel, world unchanged), hold full and rewrite begins, undo mid-rewrite, chained rewrite via Enter, new world reached, dwell, haunt (hero turns to camera, margin note), hold C compare and release, undo from the new world, chained rewrite #3, Tab focus reaches pen/undo/compare/scrub/reduce, reduced motion via R (only t = 0, 0.5, 1 observed). Run as `node tests/interaction-v2.mjs` (13 passed) and recorded with the same scenario. Waits are on state, not wall clock.

## Results

run-v2 20 passed, flow-v2 18 passed, interaction-v2 13 passed.

## Asset sources

All visuals are procedural CSS/DOM in `rewrite-v2/`. No external images, fonts or audio. Playwright (headless Chromium) and ffmpeg are used only for capture.

## Unverified

- Chinese glyph rendering on screen: no CJK font on the capture machine (`fc-list :lang=zh` empty, disk search found none), so Chinese is blank in all screenshots and the video. Chinese text presence is checked in the DOM only. The recording does not show Chinese.
- GPU performance: captures use software GL, the recording is low fps (the frames are sparse during rewrite), real GPU smoothness untested.
- Real mobile touch and real browsers other than headless Chromium.
- Pages deployment (not done, by instruction).
- Screen reader behaviour.
