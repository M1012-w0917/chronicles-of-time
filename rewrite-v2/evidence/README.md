## Interaction recorded and asserted (`tests/scenario-v2.mjs`)

Before rewrite, hold Space and release early (cancel, world unchanged), hold full and rewrite begins, undo mid-rewrite, chained rewrite via Enter, new world reached, dwell, haunt (hero turns to camera, margin note), hold C compare and release, undo from the new world, chained rewrite #3, Tab focus reaches pen/undo/compare/scrub/reduce, reduced motion via R (only t = 0, 0.5, 1 observed). Run as `node tests/interaction-v2.mjs` (13 passed) and recorded with the same scenario. Waits are on state, not wall clock.

## Results

run-v2 20 passed, flow-v2 18 passed, interaction-v2 13 passed.

## Known variation between runs

Counts in the interaction log (for example buildings left and footprints at step 3) differ between `after-interaction.txt` and `after-record.txt`, because step 3 samples mid-rewrite while headless software GL runs at varying speed. Assertions are ranges and the gone count always equals the footprint count. The end states are identical.

## Asset sources

All visuals are procedural CSS/DOM in `rewrite-v2/`. No external images, fonts or audio. Playwright (headless Chromium) and ffmpeg are used only for capture.

## Unverified

- Chinese glyph rendering on screen: no CJK font on the capture machine (`fc-list :lang=zh` empty, disk search found none, no network to fetch one), so Chinese is blank in all screenshots and the video. Chinese text presence is checked in the DOM only. The recording does not show Chinese.
- GPU performance: the added layers (up to 58 footprints, about 30 impressions, 9 creases, 12 ridges) were not measured on real hardware. captures use software GL, the recording is low fps (the frames are sparse during rewrite), real GPU smoothness untested.
- Real mobile touch and real browsers other than headless Chromium.
- Pages deployment (not done, by instruction).
- Screen reader behaviour.
