对照旧织城 Comparing with the old weave

织城志 Annals of the Weave City

桥籍 Bridge register 守桥人:顾衡 Keeper: Gu Heng

本册只记一条事实。落笔即成真,织机上的线随之抽出、改道、重新穿过。
One fact is kept here. Writing it makes it true, and the threads on the loom are pulled out, rerouted and threaded again.

长桥织城以七座石拱桥跨越白河,桥上守桥人顾衡夜夜点灯。白河从未建桥,织城沿河两岸分隔百年。
The long-bridged weave city crosses the White River on seven stone arch bridges, and on the bridges the keeper Gu Heng lights his lamp every night.No bridge was ever built over the White River, and the weave city has stood divided along its two banks for a hundred years.
