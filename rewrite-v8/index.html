窑志 Kiln Annals

桥籍 Bridge register 守桥人:陶砚 Keeper: Tao Yan

本册只记一条事实。落笔即成真,窑火随之升起,瓷胎软化,釉水改道。
One fact is kept here. Writing it makes it true: the kiln heats, the body softens and the glaze runs a new way.

七座石桥连接两岸,守桥人陶砚夜夜在桥心守灯。此地从来没有桥,只有断裂峡湾。
Seven stone bridges join the two banks, and the bridge keeper Tao Yan keeps a lamp lit at the middle of the bridge every night.There has never been a bridge here, only a broken fjord.
